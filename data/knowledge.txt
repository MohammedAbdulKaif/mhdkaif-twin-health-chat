[OVERVIEW]
Twin Health is a precision health program powered by a Whole Body Digital Twin™.
It focuses on identifying and addressing the root cause of metabolic health issues using personalized data and continuous insights.
Twin Health helps individuals improve their overall well-being through guided lifestyle changes supported by technology and coaching.

[WHOLE_BODY_DIGITAL_TWIN]
The Whole Body Digital Twin is a virtual model of an individual’s body created using biological, lifestyle, and sensor data.
It helps understand how the body responds to food, physical activity, sleep, and daily habits.
The digital twin continuously updates as the body adapts and improves over time.

[ICAPP]
ICAPP is Twin Health’s internal application platform.
It is used for coaching management, care coordination, user engagement, and operational workflows.
ICAPP enables smooth communication between users, coaches, and the care team through a centralized system.

[COACHING]
Twin Health provides personalized coaching to support sustainable lifestyle changes.
Coaching focuses on daily habits such as nutrition, physical activity, sleep routines, and consistency.
Coaches use insights from the Whole Body Digital Twin to guide users throughout their journey.

[NUTRITION_JOURNEY]
The nutrition journey in Twin Health is personalized based on how each individual’s body responds to food.
Users can log daily food intake through the Twin Health app.
Food guidance adapts over time as the digital twin learns from the user’s responses.

[CARE_TEAM]
Twin Health users have access to a dedicated care team through the app.
The care team supports users by monitoring progress and guiding engagement with the program.
All communication with the care team happens through in-app messaging.

[DASHBOARD]
The Twin Health dashboard provides a personalized view for each user.
It displays progress, insights, activity data, and engagement information.
Dashboards are unique for every user based on their individual health journey.

[PLANS]
Twin Health offers multiple subscription plans to suit different user needs.
Available plans include quarterly, half-yearly, and annual subscriptions.
Each plan provides access to the Twin Health app, personalized coaching, and ongoing program support.

[ONBOARDING]
The onboarding process in Twin Health involves collecting initial user information and lifestyle details.
This data helps create a personalized starting point for the program.
Onboarding plays a key role in building the initial Whole Body Digital Twin.

[DISCHARGE]
After the subscription period ends, users may have limited access for a short duration.
Once this period expires, the account status is changed to inactive.
This process helps manage program completion and access control.

[APP_FEATURES]
The Twin Health app allows users to track daily activities, log food intake, and view personalized insights.
Users can communicate with coaches and the care team directly through the app.
The app acts as the primary interface for engaging with the Twin Health program.

[DIABETES_REVERSAL_APPROACH]
Twin Health India focuses on addressing the root cause of metabolic conditions rather than only managing symptoms.
Lifestyle-related conditions such as Type 2 diabetes can develop due to irregular eating habits, lack of physical activity, poor sleep patterns, and stress.

Many chronic health conditions such as Type 2 diabetes, obesity, PCOD, and hypertension are linked to a disrupted or degraded metabolism.
When daily habits related to nutrition, sleep, activity, and stress are not balanced, the body’s natural metabolic pathways begin to weaken.

Twin Health works on restoring metabolic balance using the Whole Body Digital Twin™.
The digital twin analyzes individual data related to food timing, sleep quality, activity levels, and stress exposure.
Based on these insights, personalized daily recommendations support the body’s natural ability to improve metabolic health.

Twin Health follows a precision healthcare approach.
Instead of using one-size-fits-all diet charts, the program adapts to how each individual’s body responds to food, movement, and rest.
As the digital twin continues to learn, recommendations evolve to support long-term metabolic improvement.
